## Mechanism
The Combustible Arc Reactor is meant to be a self-sustaining mechanism that utilizes low-power arcs to ignite a gaseous, flammable chemical of some kind, gradually released from a porous, replaceable cartridge in a controlled and continuous manner; causing the release of a large amount of energy. This energy outburst is to be collected by a mechanism that converts the pure chemical outburst into usable electric energy. This electric output is to be fed back into the initial input mechanism that activates the arc, continuing the cycle (self-sustaining) until the central cartridge containing the gaseous chemical runs out (finite). The waste material generated from the chemical reactions will fill up the vacant space left behind by the gaseous chemical once it was released gradually in the central porous cartridge. For the C.A.R.'s self-sustaining cycle to initialize, a tiny amount of starting energy is required to activate the starter arc that initializes the reactor. The idea, explanation, and understanding of the mechanism of C.A.R. is subject to change, whether it be due to external suggestions, gaining new knowledge, and other events.
